For you

Happy Dia dos Namorados, my love.

I know things have not always felt easy lately, but I’m grateful we’ve kept choosing patience, honesty, and each other anyway. That matters to me more than I always know how to say.

Thank you for the softness you still gave me — for your letter, your note, and the care behind them. I wanted to return a little piece of that today.

I’m saving the bigger surprises for when I see you, but I didn’t want today to pass without leaving you something small and warm. Two cups, a little fire, a blanket, and a letter with my care folded into it.

My precious love, I wish you the best of days. You are special to me, and I want you to know just how much. Even from far away, I care about you deeply, and I’m so grateful that I get to keep learning you, choosing you, and looking forward to the day I can hold you properly.

Two more months, and then I get to be there with you for real.

With love,
me
